Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between single-sided and double-sided?

Single-sided light boxes are viewed from one direction only, typically wall-mounted. Double-sided boxes are illuminated and printed on both faces — ideal for hanging or freestanding signs viewed from either direction.

How much does a light box cost?

Pricing depends on size, sides and finish, so we don't publish a fixed online price. Send us your details above and we'll come back with a free, no-obligation quote.

Do I need to supply artwork?

Yes — once you submit your enquiry we'll follow up to collect your logo or graphic files (or help design one) before production begins.

Do light boxes need mains power?

Yes, all light boxes need a nearby low-voltage power connection. Let us know in your notes if you're unsure what's available at your site and we'll talk you through it.

How long does production take?

Most light box orders are ready in 5–10 working days after you approve your quote and artwork. We'll confirm a firm timeframe when we send it.

Is this form a binding order?

No. This sends us an enquiry only. We'll follow up with a formal, itemised quote before anything goes into production.
