Frequently Asked Questions

How long does production take?

Most orders are ready in 3–5 working days after you approve your quote and artwork. Larger metal or timber orders can take a little longer — we'll confirm a timeframe when we send your formal quote.

Can I use my own logo or a custom shape instead of text?

Yes. Use the text box above to get an instant estimate, then mention in your quote request that you'd like a logo or custom shape and attach your artwork — we'll price it properly for you.

Can the letters be used outdoors?

Aluminium, stainless steel and brass/copper letters are all weather-resistant and suited to outdoor use. Acrylic and PVC foam are best suited to covered or indoor applications.

Is the price shown final?

It's an instant estimate based on your text, size and material. Once we review your exact artwork we'll send a formal fixed-price quote before anything goes into production.

Do you install the letters?

We offer professional installation across Greater Newcastle, Sydney and surrounding areas — get in touch for an install quote. Outside these areas, we can ship letters anywhere in Australia for self-install, and every order comes with a paper installation template matched to your exact sign, showing the correct position for every fixing point.
