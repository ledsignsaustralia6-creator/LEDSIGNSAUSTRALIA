Frequently Asked Questions

How much do home plates cost?

Pricing depends on size, material, lighting and complexity of the design, so we don't publish a fixed online price. Send us your details above and we'll come back with a free, no-obligation quote and mock-up.

What's the difference between the plate styles?

Floating Plate stands slightly off the wall on hidden studs, casting a soft shadow line for a premium architectural look. Flush Plate mounts directly against the wall for a clean, minimal finish.

Does the LED backlighting need a separate transformer?

Yes — backlit plates require a small low-voltage transformer, which is sold separately. We'll confirm the right size and include it in your quote if needed.

Can I get a custom shape, animal or pattern?

Absolutely — koalas, kangaroos, palm trees, sports themes, geometric patterns and fully custom artwork are all possible. Just describe what you're after in the "Theme, Motif or Icon" field and we'll design around it.

Can I get a non-illuminated house plate or a custom size?

Yes — we make non-illuminated plates and fully custom dimensions too. Select "Custom Size" above or mention it in your notes and we'll quote accordingly.

How do I install my plate?

We provide a paper installation template matched to your exact plate, showing the correct position for every mounting point — making it easy to install straight and level.

How long does production take?

Most home plate orders are ready in 5–7 working days after you approve your quote. We'll confirm a firm timeframe when we send it.

Do you ship Australia-wide?

Yes — we offer free nationwide shipping on home plates.
