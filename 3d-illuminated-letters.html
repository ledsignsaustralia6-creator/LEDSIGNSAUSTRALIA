Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between front-lit and halo-lit (back-lit)?

Front-lit letters shine light directly out through the letter face for maximum brightness and visibility. Halo-lit letters — also called back-lit letters — have an opaque face and glow softly around the edges instead, creating a premium, ambient look rather than a bright direct light.

I don't know what size or material I need yet — can I still enquire?

Yes. Fill in whatever you know and tick "Not sure yet (TBA)" for the rest. We'll ask a few follow-up questions or suggest options based on your space and budget.

Do illuminated letters need mains power?

Most LED-illuminated letters need a nearby low-voltage power connection. If you're not sure what's available at your site, select "Not sure yet (TBA)" for power access and we'll talk you through it.

Can I get a sample or see finishes in person?

Yes, mention it in the notes section of the form and we'll arrange to show you finish and colour samples before you commit.

Is this form a binding order?

No. This sends us an enquiry only. We'll follow up with a formal, itemised quote before anything goes into production.
